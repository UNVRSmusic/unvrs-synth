🚀 Introducing my new web synth UNVRS-SYNTH.

Minimal, fast, intuitive, and accessible from anywhere — just open your browser and play.
No installation. Just sound. Just flow. 🎛️✨

UNVRS-SYNTH was born from the need for a simple tool to experiment with sound, create textures, or capture ideas the moment they appear.

It records everything — export to WAV or capture the entire session as MIDI from the very first note played.
No good idea gets lost.

Features:
🎹 MIDI device detection
Arpeggiator
2 experimental modes where the oscillators can really heat up 🔥 (sometimes you might need to cool things down with an Audio Engine refresh 🧯😄)
Fullscreen mode 📺
Delay & Reverb ⛰️

Try it here:
🌐 https://unvrsmusic.github.io/unvrs-synth/
